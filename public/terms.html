Eligibility, registration and approval

You must be at least 18 years old and authorized to participate in the relevant residential community or staff role. Provide accurate registration information, use your own account, keep credentials secure and notify us of suspected unauthorized access. Account creation does not guarantee approval or access to every feature.

Approval and assigned permissions determine your access. Do not impersonate another person, share an account to bypass restrictions, misrepresent your role or attempt to access private information or conversation history you are not authorized to view.

Acceptable community behavior

Use TownLoop respectfully and for lawful community purposes. You must not:

  • Harass, threaten, stalk, intimidate, discriminate against or abuse another person, or publish private personal information without permission.
  • Post scams, fraudulent listings, spam, malicious links or files, or content that violates another person's rights.
  • Create, request, distribute or facilitate child sexual abuse material, grooming, sexual exploitation, trafficking or related illegal conduct. The Child Safety Standards apply to every feature.
  • Use messaging or marketplace inquiries for abusive solicitation, deception or unwanted repeated contact.
  • Bypass approval, blocks, membership restrictions or security controls; interfere with service operation; or misuse automated requests to overload the service.

Your posts, discussions and media

You remain responsible for the content you provide, including posts, comments, messages, listings, work orders and attachments. Share only content you own or have permission to use. Do not submit another person's private or sensitive information unnecessarily.

You retain ownership of your content. You permit Seeds4Clix and its service providers to store, process, transmit and display it as needed to operate the feature you choose, deliver it to the intended audience, maintain permitted history and administer safety or legal obligations. This permission does not transfer ownership or permit unrelated commercial reuse.

Community discussions are visible to their authorized audience. Messages are limited by conversation permissions, not advertised as end-to-end encrypted. Leaving a conversation does not delete other participants' permitted history. If future contact creates a new conversation, it does not restore revoked access to an old one. Other users may retain copies they have already received.

Marketplace listings and transactions

TownLoop provides listings and messaging so participants can contact each other. Transactions are between the participating users. TownLoop does not process marketplace payments, act as the buyer or seller, inspect goods or guarantee a listing, payment, delivery or refund.

Describe items, prices and condition honestly. Offer only items you may lawfully sell or give away. Confirm payment, pickup, delivery and any other terms with the other participant. Use appropriate caution, protect financial information and report suspected fraud.

Work orders, news and reminders

Work orders support authorized maintenance communication; they are not an emergency service or a guarantee of repair timing. Provide accurate descriptions and do not enter unnecessary sensitive information. Staff roles and completion controls remain subject to community authorization.

Newsletters, RSVP events, schedules and weather can change. Confirm important arrangements with the appropriate organizer. Automated newsletter extraction and translation can omit or misstate details; review the original information and important maintenance instructions.

Medication reminders and Daily Checklists are personal organizational aids, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency monitoring or proof that a dose was taken. Follow your healthcare professional's instructions. Notifications may be delayed or unavailable because of device settings, connectivity or service interruptions. Contact local emergency services for emergencies.

Reporting, restrictions and termination

Use the in-app Report controls for inappropriate posts, listings, comments or user behavior. Authorized Admin/VIP reviewers can review reports and remove eligible reported content. Blocking a user is a personal messaging control, not a report or an account-wide suspension. For continuing concerns or a report requiring follow-up, contact Community Office through Inbox or email Seeds4Clix.

Authorized operators may restrict or terminate access when necessary to address violations, unauthorized participation, security risks or legal requirements. Account-level action requires authorized administration; filing a report does not automatically suspend the reported person. Review and escalation involve human action and are not represented as continuous real-time monitoring.

If you believe a restriction or moderation decision is incorrect, contact support@seeds4clix.com with relevant non-sensitive details. Do not send suspected illegal sexual material, passwords or login codes. Child-safety concerns follow the Child Safety Standards, including reporting to appropriate authorities where required by applicable law.

You may stop using TownLoop and request account deletion through the app or the account-deletion page. Requests require verified control and review; approval is not proof of completed deletion. The Privacy Policy describes retention, shared-content limitations and safeguards. Access restriction does not automatically erase evidence or other participants' records.
