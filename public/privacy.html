Your information in TownLoop

TownLoop is a private residential community application for adults aged 18 and over and authorized community staff. Seeds4Clix develops and operates TownLoop. Account approval and assigned roles control access to community features. TownLoop does not represent or act on behalf of a residential community's property owner or management company.

This policy explains the information used by the native app and its supporting services. Contact support@seeds4clix.com for privacy questions, corrections or deletion assistance.

Medication and Daily Checklist entries stay on your device. They are not synced to TownLoop's Firebase databases. Posts, messages, work orders and other content you submit to community features are stored through our online services.

Information you provide

  • Registration and sign-in: Email address, account identifier and authentication details managed through Firebase Authentication. If you use Google Sign-In, account information such as your name, email and profile image is provided through that sign-in flow.
  • Profile and approval: Name, community address, phone number, profile photo, approval status, role and recorded acceptance of app terms. Where provided through profile controls, records can also include date of birth, marital status and emergency-contact details. Provide someone else's contact information only with their permission.
  • Community participation: Discussion posts, comments, replies, likes, marketplace listings and inquiries, newsletter contributions, RSVP participation and related timestamps.
  • Messages: Conversation participants, message content, attachments and available sent, delivered or read acknowledgments. Group membership and permitted message history are managed by the messaging service.
  • Work orders: Request descriptions, contact/address information, photos, staff responses, assignments and completion history.
  • Reports and account requests: Reported item or user identifiers, the reason and context you submit, moderation decisions, account-deletion requests and associated review or processing records.

Photos, videos and documents you select are uploaded when you submit them through the corresponding feature. These files may contain identifying information or embedded metadata; avoid including details that are unnecessary for the intended audience.

How information is used and who can see it

We use information to authenticate accounts, verify approval, display profiles, operate community features, deliver messages and notifications, respond to maintenance requests, process reports and account requests, prevent abuse and diagnose technical problems.

Profile fields made available in Friends & Neighbors can be seen by other authorized users. Additional profile or emergency-contact information is subject to the app's role-based access. Community posts and listings are shared with their authorized community audience. Work orders are available to the requesting resident and authorized staff under the applicable permissions.

Private conversations are intended for authorized participants. Newly added group members receive only the history their membership permits. Reporting a person does not automatically grant reviewers unrestricted access to private conversations. Authorized backend operators may access information when necessary for support, security, moderation or legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information is processed by the service providers described below and may be disclosed where necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests or address safety and security concerns. Sharing content with another user cannot prevent that person from saving or redistributing it outside TownLoop.

Medication records and Daily Checklist

Medication names, dose and bottle counts, reminder settings, dose-completion records and Daily Checklist tasks are saved in account-scoped storage on the device. These entries are not automatically uploaded to Firebase, sent to Gemini or shared with staff or other residents. They are not a cloud medical record or a cross-device backup service.

Local protection depends on the installed app version and device capabilities. Supported versions use protected storage; older versions can use device storage without app-level encryption. Signing out can preserve these records for that account on the same device. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove them, subject to operating-system storage and backup behavior.

If you copy health or checklist information into a message, work order, post or uploaded document, that information is transmitted and stored as part of that feature. Reminders are organizational aids, not medical advice, emergency monitoring or proof that medication was taken.

Device permissions and technical information

Notification features use your operating-system permission status, push token, installation identifier, platform, category preferences and notification/unread state. Eligible notifications cover Private Inbox, Marketplace messages, Discussion activity and Work Order updates. You can change the four preferences in Account → Notifications and control system authorization, previews, sounds and badges in device settings.

The app uses the system photo/file picker and can request camera access when you choose to take a photo. Declining optional media or notification access does not prevent ordinary use of the rest of TownLoop. The weather feature requests forecasts for configured community coordinates; it does not request your phone's GPS location.

Firebase, Expo and other network services can process connection information, including IP addresses, request times, app or platform details and diagnostic/error records, to provide and protect the service. Authentication tokens, local preferences and cached media/documents can also be stored on the device. TownLoop does not require access to your device address book to provide its community directory.

Services that process information

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: Authentication, Firestore databases, Storage, Cloud Functions and Hosting provide account, community-content, file, backend and public-page services. Some historical account-related records remain in a secured legacy database used by authorized backend services.
  • Expo and platform push services: Expo provides app updates and push delivery, with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android and Apple Push Notification service on iOS where configured. Notification payloads are designed to avoid private message bodies, but device lock-screen visibility depends on your settings.
  • Google Gemini: When an authorized editor analyzes a newsletter, its PDF content is processed by Gemini through TownLoop's authenticated backend to extract RSVP events and highlights for human review. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in a newsletter submitted for analysis.
  • Google translation services: When an authorized user requests a Work Order translation or translates a Crew response, the selected text is sent to the configured Google translation service. Existing server translation routes can also use Gemini. Local dictionary translations do not require sending the text to a provider. Check important translated instructions against the original.
  • Open-Meteo: Supplies forecasts for configured community coordinates. Requests also convey ordinary network connection information.

Provider terms govern their processing, locations and retention; a zero-retention or no-model-training guarantee is not made here. Gemini's treatment of submitted content can depend on the service tier and applicable terms. See Firebase privacy information, Gemini API terms, Expo privacy information and Open-Meteo terms. Third-party links and services have their own policies.

Retention and account deletion

Account and community records are kept while needed to operate the service, maintain permitted conversation history, process requests or meet documented safety, security and legal obligations. Leaving a conversation removes your access; it does not immediately erase the remaining participants' history. Do not assume that an inactive conversation or an unpublished upload has already been purged.

Request deletion from the app's Account Deletion screen or follow the instructions on our account-deletion page. If you cannot sign in, email support@seeds4clix.com from your sign-in address. We must verify account control before acting; never email a password or login code.

A request is reviewed by an authorized Admin/VIP other than the requester. Sending a request or receiving approval does not mean deletion is already complete. The existing workflow targets review within 3 business days, escalation after 7 calendar days and personal-data processing within 7 calendar days after approval. These are processing targets, not guaranteed completion deadlines; identity, ownership, evidence or technical safeguards can require further review.

Eligible profiles, account records and owned content are removed or redacted through the deletion workflow. Other people's content, shared files, necessary anonymized maintenance history and records subject to documented legal or evidence holds may require separate handling. Minimal account-linked processing and cleanup receipts use an original-request-based 60-calendar-day retention window; retries do not restart it. A request must not be described as complete while unresolved personal-data exceptions remain.

Live deletion does not instantly erase restricted recovery copies or provider logs. The last verified Storage configuration provided seven-day soft-delete recovery; backup/recovery copies and provider logs follow their applicable settings. A permanently offline device cannot be remotely wiped. Contact us if you need the status or remaining scope of a particular request.

Security

TownLoop uses authenticated access, account approval, role and membership checks, and server-side authorization for sensitive operations. These protections reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Private messages are not represented as end-to-end encrypted. Some historical media links may remain usable by someone who already possesses the link; removing a post, leaving a conversation or revoking an account does not by itself prove that every existing file link has stopped working.

Protect your device and credentials, report suspected unauthorized access and avoid sharing private file links. Our public legal pages do not require sign-in and contain no advertising or analytics scripts; Hosting still processes normal web requests.

Your choices and privacy contact

Use profile editing, notification settings, local reminder controls and report/block controls for their respective functions. You can contact Seeds4Clix to request access, corrections, deletion or help with applicable privacy rights. Verification may be required to protect the account and other residents.

TownLoop is intended for adults 18+, not children. If you believe a child is using the service or that child-safety information needs attention, contact the Seeds4Clix Safety Team. Relevant information must be handled according to applicable safety and legal obligations.
