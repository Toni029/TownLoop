What TownLoop does

TownLoop provides approved residents and authorized staff with community news, newsletters, RSVP events, recurring activity reminders, maintenance requests, a discussion feed, marketplace listings and private or staff conversations. Accounts and features are subject to community approval and role-based access.

Account information and community content

Firebase Authentication handles email/password and configured Google sign-in. Account and profile records can include your name, email, community address, phone number, profile picture, emergency-contact details, approval status, role and recorded terms acceptance. Provide another person’s contact information only with their permission.

When you use the corresponding features, TownLoop stores posts, comments, likes, listings, messages, work orders and status updates, RSVP participation and selected media or documents in Firebase services. Content is available to the intended community audience, conversation participants or authorized staff as appropriate. Messages are not represented as end-to-end encrypted.

Device information and permissions

TownLoop uses notification permission, a device push token and notification state/preferences to deliver eligible Inbox, Marketplace, Discussion reply and Work Order notifications. You can change the four categories under Account → Notifications and control OS authorization in device settings. Delivery, banners and badges depend on platform settings and configuration.

When you choose to attach media, the app uses the system picker and can request camera access for taking a photo. Declining optional permission does not remove your account. The weather feature uses configured community coordinates rather than requesting your phone’s GPS location.

Medication reminders and local records

Medication and checklist reminders are account-specific device records, not a cloud medical-record service. Storage protection depends on the installed app version and device capabilities; ordinary sign-out can preserve local reminders. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove them. Information about health that you put into a message, work order, post or upload can leave the device as part of that feature.

Reminders are organizational tools, not medical advice, emergency services or a guarantee that a medicine was taken. Follow your healthcare professional’s instructions.

Services processing information

Google Firebase provides authentication, database, file and backend services. Expo provides app updates and the configured push-delivery service. Open-Meteo provides weather. Network requests can include connection and technical information processed by those providers.

When an authorized editor analyzes a newsletter, its PDF is sent through TownLoop’s secure backend to Google Gemini to extract events and highlights for human review. Maintenance translation can send request text or comments to Google translation services or a Gemini fallback. Avoid unnecessary sensitive material and verify important AI/translation results against the original.

Provider processing locations, contracts, retention/training settings and final Data Safety classifications need owner confirmation. No promise of zero provider retention or exclusion from model training is made. A native crash-reporting SDK and new scheduled backup/PITR settings are proposed, not activated by this draft; any later deployment needs corresponding disclosure review.

Retention and account deletion

You can submit a verified request from the app’s Account Deletion screen or use the external request page. A request does not immediately delete your account or sign you out. It is reviewed by an independent Admin/VIP; you cannot approve your own request.

The existing approved policy targets review within 3 business days, escalation after 7 calendar days and personal-data processing within 7 calendar days of approval. Ordinary retained records and account-linked processing receipts have an original-request-based 60-calendar-day limit. These are existing TownLoop policy targets, not newly imposed legal deadlines. The current worker is deployed but its policy-receipt safety gate is paused pending separate authorization; do not interpret approval alone as proof that processing has completed. Confirm this status and operational handling before publication.

Eligible personal profiles, owned content and account records are removed or redacted under the approved workflow. Shared conversations and operational records may preserve other people’s content while removing the requesting resident’s personal information. Reported evidence, legal/security obligations, ambiguous ownership and shared attachments require a documented decision and applicable retention handling; they must not be silently erased or described as already deleted.

Application Storage currently has seven-day soft-delete recovery. Both Firestore databases currently report one-hour version history without PITR or scheduled backups; proposed recovery changes are not active. Deleting live application records does not immediately erase restricted recovery copies or provider logs. An offline device or older app copy cannot be remotely erased until it receives usable authoritative information. Final recovery, exception and provider-log disclosures require approval.
