Last updated: September 2026

We only collect information necessary to provide and improve our app experience:

We use collected data solely for legitimate app functionality, including:

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We utilize trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements:

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services. You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at the email listed below.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.
