Child Safety Standards

TownLoop is a private residential community app intended for adults and approved community staff. Child-safety protections apply to all content and conduct, whether or not a child has an account.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

TownLoop prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), and content or behavior that promotes, facilitates or normalizes the sexual abuse, exploitation or endangerment of a child.

This applies to profiles, discussion posts, comments, marketplace listings, images, videos, attachments, conversations and all other uses of TownLoop. It includes:

  • Creating, uploading, storing, requesting, offering, distributing or linking to CSAM, including sexually exploitative depictions involving children and computer-generated or manipulated material depicting child sexual abuse.
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation, inappropriate sexual communication with or directed at a child, or arranging sexual contact with a child.
  • Sexual exploitation, sexualization, sex trafficking or the commercial sexual exploitation of children.
  • Sextortion, threats, blackmail or coercion involving a child's actual or alleged intimate images or sexual activity.
  • Using TownLoop to locate, recruit or contact children for abuse or to evade action taken against prohibited conduct.

TownLoop's adults-only, private-community purpose does not permit any of this conduct. A report is not dismissed simply because an account is approved or the reported person is an adult.

How to report a concern in TownLoop

  1. Use the Report flag on the relevant discussion post, marketplace listing or comment. For a concern about a person or private conversation, use Report user in that person's profile or conversation header.
  2. Choose Unsafe or Other. In the optional context field, clearly identify it as a child-safety concern and provide enough non-explicit context to locate it, such as the relevant account, item, conversation and date/time.
  3. Tap Send report. The report is submitted to the community office's Admin/VIP review queue. If sending fails, the app shows an error; you can retry or contact the child-safety team below.

Do not download, save, forward, re-upload or email suspected CSAM. Do not paste private conversation contents into the report form. Report the location and nature of the concern without redistributing prohibited material. The team may request limited additional context through an appropriate process.

You may also contact support@seeds4clix.com. Include “TownLoop child safety concern” and non-explicit identifying details. Do not send passwords, verification codes or suspected CSAM as email attachments.

If a concern continues or new information becomes available after an earlier report was reviewed, use Inbox → Community Office to request further child-safety review, or email the child-safety team. Repeating a report about the same item or person may return the earlier report rather than create a new review. Do not assume that a repeat submission has reopened the case.

Personal Block user controls are separate from reporting. Blocking someone is not a report to the office and is not an account-level suspension.

Review, removal and escalation

  1. Review: Authorized community Admin/VIP reviewers assess submitted reports and the content they are permitted to access. A report does not grant unrestricted access to private conversations. Child-safety concerns are escalated to the Seeds4Clix Safety Team, TownLoop's responsible child-safety team.
  2. Containment and content removal: Confirmed prohibited content is removed or access to it is restricted. Admin/VIP can remove reported posts, listings and comments through the review queue. The child-safety team coordinates any additional account, message or file actions through authorized administrative tools. A case must not be treated as contained until related media access has also been checked.
  3. Account restrictions: Depending on the concern and evidence, the authorized TownLoop operator restricts or suspends involved accounts and takes appropriate action to prevent further abuse. Account-level restrictions are coordinated by the child-safety team; they are not an automatic result of submitting a report.
  4. Evidence and authorities: The child-safety team coordinates secure, restricted handling of information that must be preserved under applicable law. Where required by applicable law, confirmed CSAM and other reportable child-exploitation concerns are reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline or the relevant regional authority. TownLoop cooperates with valid requests from authorities. Preservation does not mean leaving prohibited material accessible to users.

These steps require human review and authorized operator action. TownLoop does not claim automated CSAM detection, automatic account suspension, automatic authority reporting or continuous real-time monitoring.

Immediate danger: Contact your local emergency services or law enforcement immediately if a child may be in immediate danger. Do not wait for TownLoop's report review. You can also submit a concern directly to NCMEC's CyberTipline.
