What happens next

Submitting the request does not immediately delete your account or sign you out of the native app. Admin/VIP reviews the verified request independently. Existing targets are review within 3 business days, escalation after 7 calendar days and personal-data processing within 7 calendar days after approval; ordinary retained records and receipts follow the original 60-calendar-day limit. The deployed worker currently remains paused behind its policy-receipt safeguard. This status and staffed handling must be confirmed before publication.

Eligible personal account data is removed or redacted under the existing workflow. Other participants’ content, documented evidence/retention obligations and shared attachments are preserved as applicable. Recovery copies and offline devices are not immediately erased.
