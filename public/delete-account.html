Request TownLoop account deletion

Verify your identity and submit a deletion request for your signed-in account. Submitting the request does not immediately delete your account or disable access. Admin/VIP reviews each verified request. Approval queues processing under the explicitly approved retention policy; this page never deletes your account immediately. Shared conversations, community content, work orders and safety records may be retained under an approved policy.

Retention decisions are pending owner approval. Requests can remain pending review. This page does not promise an unapproved completion date or delete shared history automatically. Review the privacy policy.
